## What
Removed the written notes in `docs/` that the report replaces, and every `.md` file under
`docs/results/` and `docs/figures/` (48 tracked `.md` files in `docs/` before → 1 after:

covered by the report: methodology and protocol in Ch. 3, results and interpretation in

  `docs/literature/` (7 files).
- Deleted 35 `.md` tables / captions in `docs/results/` and `docs/figures/` and added them
  to `.gitignore` (`/docs/results/*.md`, `/docs/figures/**/*.md`). The scripts still write
  them locally; removing the writers would have meant refactoring 9 files plus their tests

- Kept: `docs/results/*.csv`, `docs/figures/**` images (the report loads them through
  `\graphicspath`), `docs/experiment_log.csv` (appended by `utils/runlog.py`),
  `docs/data_audit.json` (read by the EDA notebook), `docs/dataset_stats.json`,
  `docs/dataset_card.md`.

## Result
