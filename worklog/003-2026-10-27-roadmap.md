## What
Designed the full plan of action from 27 Oct to 17 Nov and saved it as `ROADMAP.md`. It has
phases, a task checklist, review deliverables, scope controls, risks and a decision log.

## Why
There are only 3 weeks and three reviews (3, 10 and 17 Nov), and it's a solo project. Every
rubric row has to be covered on time, and it has to be clear what can be cut if time runs short.

## How
The plan was proposed in chat, reviewed by the user, then revised. User decisions:
- **Look at other datasets** before committing to Jinan/Hangzhou. This added Phase 1, a
  dataset survey with a comparison table, **due 31 Oct**. The user wrote "31st Nov", which
  doesn't exist; we took it as 31 Oct, before Review 1.
- **Single-agent baseline** = a shared-parameter DQN with no coordination. A truly
  centralized agent is infeasible: 4¹² joint actions on Jinan.
- **Solo project:** no team roles. Added scope controls. Never cut the main comparison,
  ablation, report, slides or demo. Shrink robustness and generalization first.
- **File:** `ROADMAP.md` at the root, linked from `CLAUDE.md`. Added the 31 Oct milestone
  to `PRD.md`.

## Result
11 phases (0–10) with checkbox tasks mapped to rubric rows R1–R8. Also added an effort
estimate for going from single-agent to multi-agent:
- Shared DQN → IDQN: about 2–4 hours.
- Adding neighbor attention: about 1 day.
- Adding the ensemble and gate: about half a day.
